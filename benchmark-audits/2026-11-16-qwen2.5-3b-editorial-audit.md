## What this run establishes

1. The updated prompt and candidate/editor pipeline can produce valid English outputs reliably on Qwen 2.5 3B.
2. The editor selected candidate 2 in 7 of 8 cases. This is worth testing for candidate-order/temperature preference; it is not proof of a position bias by itself.
3. The central failure is not output formatting. It is weak comedic reasoning: weak premises, weak payoffs, irrelevant invented details, and low originality.
4. The current 3B default does **not** meet the project's stated quality goal on this diagnostic set. Do not advertise it as “viral” or “excellent.”

## Decision

Keep the default unchanged until a controlled comparison demonstrates that another locally runnable model is materially better. The Qwen 3.5 9B run is a separate candidate evaluation; it must be judged from its own outputs, not assumed to be better because it is larger.

For external validation, at least two real reviewers should independently complete `human-review.csv` while blind to the model identity. This assistant audit cannot substitute for them.
